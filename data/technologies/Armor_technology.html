      No Step Back replaces the basic armor technology system with a more modular system that feeds into the tank designer. Rather than researching dedicated vehicles, the new system focuses on researching only the chassis for entire classes of vehicles, with a few armor technologies also unlocking new armor and special modules. Importantly, the armaments used by tanks in the tank designer, which are also used to dictate which specialized roles a given armor variant can take, are researched as part of artillery technologies instead, and many powerful special modules require research in other technology trees, such as Support companies technology or Engineering technology.

      With the introduction of special projects in Götterdämmerung and the accompanying Patch 1.15, all armor technologies now also generate land warfare breakthrough progress while being researched. Armor technologies are also particularly intertwined with the  Land Cruiser land warfare special project.

      Light Chassis[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      These technologies serve to unlock new chassis that can be used for Light tanks, Light self-propelled anti-air, Light self-propelled artillery, and Light tank destroyers, as well as the Armored recon, Light flame tank, and, with Arms Against Tyranny, Airborne light armor support companies. They also help unlock amphibious chassis for amphibious designs, and their chassis can be used for Light amphibious tank designs.

      Light chassis tend to be cheap and make for faster and more reliable vehicles, especially in the earlygame, but they also tend to have little armor, far less breakthrough than their heavier counterparts, and far more limited armament options, especially in the later years, when they act far better as more specialized combat support vehicles or in support companies than as frontline tanks.

      Amphibious Chassis[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      This pair of technologies first unlocks a rather limited chassis for Amphibious Tanks and then unlocks a special module that can be used to make more flexible Light, Medium, and Heavy amphibious tank designs. These units tend to be more expensive than their non-amphibious counterparts, but they are far better in wet terrain and at performing naval invasions. Their designs can also use heavier weapons that are normally reserved for without being forced to be classified as such, allowing them to retain more breakthrough and benefit from certain land doctrine bonuses that are given only to tanks and not to armor variants.

      All amphibious tanks count as special forces, benefitting from general bonuses given to special forces units, but also being limited by a country's special forces capacity.

      Medium Chassis[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      These technologies serve to unlock new chassis that can be used for Medium tanks, Medium self-propelled anti-air, Medium self-propelled artillery, and Medium tank destroyers, as well as the Medium flame tank support company. They can also be used for Medium amphibious tanks. In the late game, they can also be used to unlock modern chassis that can be used for Modern tanks, Modern self-propelled anti-air, Modern self-propelled artillery, and Modern tank destroyers.

      Medium chassis vehicles tend to strike a good balance between cost and effectiveness. While not as cheap as their light chassis cousins, the improvements they offer in stats and armament options can be far greater relative to their cost increase, regularly making them the best choice in terms of stats-per-cost. Their heavy chassis cousins can still absolutely outclass them in terms of one-on-one combat performance, but are far more expensive and so are far harder to field in critical mass numbers compared to vehicles based on medium chassis.

      Heavy Chassis[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      These technologies serve to unlock new chassis that can be used for Heavy tanks, Heavy self-propelled anti-air, Heavy self-propelled artillery, and Heavy tank destroyers, as well as the Heavy flame tank support company. They can also be used for Heavy amphibious tanks. In the late game, they can also be used to unlock super-heavy chassis that can be used for Super-heavy tank, Super-heavy self-propelled anti-air, Super-heavy self-propelled artillery, and Super-heavy tank destroyer support company; with Götterdämmerung, these can also lead to the development of equipment for the monstrous  Land Cruiser support company. Heavy chassis technologies can act as alternative prerequisites to medium chassis technologies for unlocking modern chassis that can be used for Modern tanks, Modern self-propelled anti-air, Modern self-propelled artillery, and Modern tank destroyers.

      Heavy chassis vehicles tend to be amazing in terms of combat effectiveness, but have incredibly high production and resource costs. They also tend to be much slower than their lighter cousins and receive greater terrain penalties than them in more adverse conditions. Still, on the attack on the battlefield, they are essentially peerless, and opponents who face these vehicles will either have to match them with their own or find a less conventional way of dealing with them.
