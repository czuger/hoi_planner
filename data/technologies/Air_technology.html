      By Blood Alone replaces the basic air technology system with a more modular system that feeds into the aircraft designer. Rather than researching aircraft within different roles, the new system splits up research based on the airframes from which plane designs are built and the modules that are to go into said airframes, including weaponry, engines, and even some special modules.

      With the introduction of special projects in Götterdämmerung and the accompanying Patch 1.15, all air technologies now also generate aerodynamics and avionics breakthrough progress while being researched.
