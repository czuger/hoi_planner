      Support companies technology unlocks and enhances support companies that can supplement divisions when assigned to them. It also includes technologies that unlock various trains and railway guns.

      For an in-depth overview of support companies, please see the corresponding section on their dedicated page.

They also provide various terrain movement bonuses and are some of the few support companies whose movement speeds can decrease the speed of their divisions.

      Signal[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      These technologies unlock and enhance Signal company companies and their variants. These support companies focus on increasing their divisions' Initiative, which allows them to gain planning bonus faster and be more likely to reinforce into available combat width in ongoing battles. The latter can be especially helpful when performing naval invasions or when trying to hold a defensive line with multiple divisions in the same province.

      These technologies are also classified as both motorized and electronics technologies, and can therefore benefit from corresponding military industrial organizations and technology bonuses.
