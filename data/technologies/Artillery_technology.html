      Artillery technology unlocks technologies related to different types of artillery, anti-air guns, and anti-tank guns. There are four larger branches in the tree: the artillery branch, the anti-air branch, the anti-tank branch, and the rocket artillery branch. The starting technology for each major branch unlocks a new piece of equipment, a line combat battalion, and a corresponding support company that can be used in templates in the division designer.

      All of these technologies are interlinked with special projects, providing breakthrough progress in one or more categories while being researched as well as benefiting benefitting from any scientist engaged in passive research in their fields. Most are tied to land warfare projects, indicated by green ribbons in the lower right-hand corner of their icons. Some of them are tied to aerodynamics and avionics projects, indicated by red ribbons in the lower right-hand corner of their icons.

      Anti-air[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.
      Back to Artillery research tree

      Anti-air artillery has a very powerful air attack, allowing it to shoot down enemy aircraft. Their hard attack and piercing capabilities are significantly better than those of standard artillery, but inferior to anti-tank, and their soft attack is a fraction of standard artillery. This makes them better than standard artillery at attacking lightly armored vehicles, like motorized and mechanized infantry, but relatively poor against infantry (though they will still do a serviceable job). Units that deal air attack damage also help to counter enemy air superiority in the strategic region they are located in and shoot down enemy Close Air Support.

      Anti-air technologies are all tied to both the land warfare and aerodynamics and avionics special project categories, generating breakthrough progress in those categories when they are being researched and benefitting from scientists engaged in passive research in either category.

      Anti-tank[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.
      Back to Artillery research tree

      Anti-tank artillery deals a lot of hard damage and has high piercing, making them excel in taking out vehicles on the ground. However, their low soft attack means they are not very effective against softer targets, such as infantry.

      Anti-tank technologies are all tied to the land warfare special project category, generating breakthrough progress in that category when they are being researched and benefitting from scientists engaged in passive research in that category.

      Wise commanders should note that 40%[1] of a division's piercing comes from the piercing of the brigade or support company with the highest piercing value in that division. Just a single line brigade or support company that uses anti-tank guns can therefore be incredibly valuable, as they can ensure that the division can pierce enemy armored divisions.

      Rocket artillery[modifier | modifier le wikicode]

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.
      Back to Artillery research tree

      Although slightly more expensive, rocket artillery tends to deal more soft damage and have much greater breakthrough than normal artillery, but is even less effective against armored vehicles.

      Rocket artillery technologies are all tied to the aerodynamics and avionics special project category, generating breakthrough progress in that category when they are being researched and benefitting from scientists engaged in passive research in that category.
