      Engineering technology

      De Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki
      Aller à la navigation Aller à la recherche

      This is a community maintained wiki. If you spot a mistake, please help with fixing it.

      Engineering technology unlocks technologies related to computing, radios, radar, fortifications, nuclear weapons, rockets, and rocket engines. Like industry technologies, engineering technologies tend to provide general bonuses on the country level, but unlike industry technologies, these bonuses tend to be ones related to research speed or unit stats, not economic benefits (at least, not directly). Without La Résistance, it also includes technologies that affect encryption and decryption.

      These technologies are heavily interlinked with special projects, with many of them either requiring or unlocking certain projects. Some engineering technologies are also tied to specific special project categories, providing breakthrough progress in them when being researched and benefitting from any scientist engaged in passive research in the field. Notably, technologies with a red ribbon in the bottom right-hand corner of their icons are tied to aerodynamics and avionics projects, those with a yellow ribbon in the bottom right-hand corner of their icons are tied to nuclear research projects, and those with both red and yellow ribbons (e.g. radar technologies) are tied to both special project categories.
